Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address and password. When you use Spendly, we also store the expenses you log, including amount, category, date and description.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to provide Spendly's features, such as showing your spending by category and time period. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your expense records for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the Spendly database. We keep it for as long as your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time, and we will remove it within a reasonable period.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log basic request details such as your IP address. We do not share your expense data with any other third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at [support email].
